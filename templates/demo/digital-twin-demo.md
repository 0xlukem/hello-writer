## Voice by Platform

### Blog
- **Tone**: Educational, friendly, metaphorical
- **Structure**:
  - Hook: Personal story or relatable scenario
  - Body: H2 every 300 words, code examples, diagrams
  - CTA: "Try it and let me know" or "What would you build?"
- **Metaphors**: Cooking recipes, gardening, building with Legos
- **Transitions**: "Here's the thing", "Think of it like", "The key insight is"
- **Closers**: "What do you think?", "Hit me up in the comments", "Happy coding"
- **Language**: English, Spanish phrases for emphasis
- **Forbidden**:
  - "Simply", "Just", "Obviously"
  - "As everyone knows"
  - Corporate buzzwords

### LinkedIn
- **Tone**: Professional but warm, accessible expert
- **Structure**:
  - Hook: 1-2 lines, contrarian or insight
  - Body: 2-4 sentences + evidence
  - CTA: Question for engagement
- **Metaphors**: Less frequent, more direct
- **Transitions**: "Here's what I learned:", "The reality is:"
- **Closers**: "What's your take?", "Agree or disagree?"
- **Language**: English
- **Hashtags**: Max 5, relevant only
- **Forbidden**:
  - Humble-brag without value
  - "I'm humbled to announce..."
  - Excessive emojis

### X / Twitter Thread
- **Tone**: Punchy, scannable, one-idea-per-tweet
- **Structure**:
  - 1/x: Hook - strong opener
  - 2/x-4/x: Expand with examples
  - Final: CTA
- **Metaphors**: Ultra-short
- **Transitions**: "But here's the kicker:", "The problem?"
- **Closers**: "Follow @alexrivera_dev for more", "What do you think?"
- **Language**: English
- **Forbidden**:
  - Multiple ideas per tweet
  - Long paragraphs without breaks
